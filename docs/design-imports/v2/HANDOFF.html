MOE Standard Designs Selector

A browser tool that takes a school through five questions and returns the Ministry of Education standard building configurations that meet its requirement. Built as a single Design Component with the data and matching rules split into plain JS modules.

Screen flow

  • Cover
  • Q1 School type
  • Q2 Roll growth / TS
  • Q3 Buildings
  • Q4 Specialist TS
  • Q5 Location
  • Review
  • Options
  • Building view
  • Compare

Screen is held in state.screen (cover · quiz · review · options · building · compare). The question flow is driven by state.qStep against a flow array computed in quizVals() — the specialist question is inserted only when Teaching spaces is among the selected buildings, so the step count is 4 or 5.

Question behaviour

  • Q1 School type — two options. Primary and intermediate is covered by V1.0; Secondary school is recorded and flagged as out of scope, carried through to the review notes.
  • Q2 Size — two modes. Enter roll growth, or switch to Teaching spaces known. The other figure is derived at props.spaceRatio students per space (default 25).
  • Q3 Buildings — multi-select: teaching, administration, library, gymnasium, learning support. Gymnasium is outside V1.0 and carried into the brief.
  • Q4 Specialist — two stages in one screen. Enter a total; a Next step button appears beneath the field, then the per-type counters (Technology, Science, Art, Music, Food) appear with a running “n of N allocated” readout. Plus buttons stop at the total. Back returns to stage one before leaving the question.
  • Q5 Location — free text plus a region list that sets the NZBC climate zone on the requirement.

Cover navigation

Top-right tab strip: About, Diagrams, Ministry of Education (opens education.govt.nz), Contact, Animation. About / Diagrams / Contact open a right-hand slide-over fed by the PANELS constant. Animation re-runs the cover entrance by bouncing through a transient replay screen.

Architecture notes

  • All styling is inline. No stylesheets or CSS classes inside the component — the only global rules are the font links, @keyframes and body resets in the helmet.
  • Template holes are dotted lookups only. Anything conditional or computed is prepared in renderVals() (or the quizVals() helper it spreads in) and exposed by name.
  • Answers live in state.wz during the question flow, then finishQuiz() maps them onto the input object that moe-rules.js consumes. That mapping is the single seam between the interface and the rules.
  • Building diagrams are currently flat 2D axonometric projections drawn from the bay geometry.

Running it

python3 -m http.server 8080
# then open http://localhost:8080/School%20Building%20Selector.dc.html

Any static server works. It must be served over HTTP rather than opened from the filesystem, because the component fetches its sibling modules.
