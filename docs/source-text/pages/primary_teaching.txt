Great schools, great spaces
Standard layouts for primary school
teaching spaces
August 2026
Version 1.0

Great schools need great spaces. Warm, dry, safe and light-filled classrooms.
Areas that flex to how your teachers like to teach. Buildings and spaces that
feel good and belong to your school and your community. Buildings that are
easy to maintain.

We have collaborated closely with architects, engineers, builders, and
educators to develop designs that achieve all this — consistently and
affordably. We have called these standard designs. They meet all New
Zealand building requirements, make sense for most schools, and respond to
your school’s identity, your site, and the way your students learn.

Our plan is to keep improving these standard designs. As we gather feedback
from schools, builders, and designers, we will review and update our standard
designs to ensure they work for everyone.

Introduction

Purpose

What this document includes

This document is part of our standard
design guidance suite. It provides detailed
technical information for schools to support
discussions about their teaching and
classroom needs. It also provides a clear
and consistent starting point for schools,
designers and builders undertaking school
property projects.

This document includes a range of standard
layouts for primary school teaching spaces.
Each layout includes:

These standard layouts for primary school
teaching spaces bring together insights
from educators, principals, and designers
to create spaces that are functional and
flexible.

How to use this document
We recommend using this document
early in the design process to support
conversations about selecting the right
teaching space layout, and to confirm who
is supplying which elements in your design
project.

› a general arrangement plan and 3D
views of each layout
› indicative furniture arrangements,
including items that are expected to be
school-supplied*
› elements provided as part of the build,
such as finishes, fixtures and joinery*
› a room data sheet showing the details
and quantity of items included in the
drawings.
*Note: Elements expected to be schoolsupplied are marked in blue on the isometric
drawings and room sheets. Items supplied as
part of the build are marked in black.

Occupancy and space design
Each standard layout includes an indicative
upper occupancy number. This provides a
guide to the limitations of each space, but is
not considered a target or a maximum.

Bracing walls and openings between
teaching spaces
In some of our single-storey standard
designs, bracing walls are needed to
support the structure. The amount of
bracing wall needed is indicated by red
coloured measurements annotated with a
‘BR’ on the relevant drawings.
Provided that a teaching space has the
total amount of bracing wall required in
metres, the bracing walls can be placed
where they best suit a school’s teaching
preferences.
Openings between teaching spaces can be
closed with a door (with selections available
from the door schedule in this document), or
they can be left open, depending on a
school’s preferences.
