Great schools need great spaces. Welcoming, safe, and well-designed
gymnasiums (gyms) and halls that support a wide range of activities. Spaces
that adapt to how your school teaches, plays, performs, and gathers. Buildings
and facilities that are part of your school and your community, while also being
practical and easy to maintain.

We have collaborated closely with architects, engineers, builders, and
educators to develop designs that achieve all this — consistently and
affordably.

We have called these standard designs. They meet all New Zealand building
requirements and work for most schools. They are designed to reflect your
school’s identity, site, and the different ways that your staff, students and
community use these spaces.

We plan to keep improving these standard designs. As we gather feedback
from schools, builders, and designers, we will review and update our standard
designs to ensure they work for everyone.

Using standard designs means your school

‘Standard’ does not mean the same.
Within these design options, your school
has choices — about building placement,
how spaces are arranged, what
materials best reflect your community
and kaupapa, and how your buildings
flow and connect with the rest of your
environment.

› a clearer process from planning through
to handover
› open and clear communication from us
at every stage of the process
› faster delivery because the key design
work has already been done
› high-quality learning spaces
› layout options that we know work well,
based on what we’ve heard and learned
that best supports student learning.
Some parts of the design — such as
structural systems, roofing, acoustic design,
ceiling heights and window dimensions —
are standard because they help us to better
manage cost, quality and delivery time.
But other parts can be chosen by you, so
the final building works for your site, your
students and your way of working.

Standard designs are a series of modular
building blocks that can be added to
and organised to create a building that
is suitable for a school’s needs. This gives
schools choice and flexibility based on
proven designs to ensure buildings are well
planned, consistent, and delivered efficiently.
The gym and hall modules provide a flexible,
multi-purpose space with extra room for
equipment, storage, and support functions
as needed. Amenity modules can provide
changing rooms, toilets, administration, or
support spaces, depending on the agreed
project scope and what already exists on
your site.

Planning for your site
Every school site is different. During
planning, we work with you to look at how
a new building will fit into your wider school
environment.
This includes:
› connections to other learning spaces
› access to outdoor areas
› sunlight, shade, trees, and wind
protection
› existing buildings and heritage features
› services and drainage
› ground conditions
› future development of your school.
Planning also considers how the building
can reflect your school’s identity and
kaupapa, and support mātauranga Māori
where appropriate. The aim is to make the
standard design work well in your school
and wider community, both now and into
the future.

Choosing gym layouts
Gymnasiums can be arranged in different
ways to support your school’s physical
education, sport, recreation, and wellbeing
needs. These standard layouts show a
range of options, including different court
configurations, seating arrangements,
amenities, storage areas, and flexible zones
that support a variety of activities and
events.
Your project team will work with you to help
choose the layout option, or combination of
options, that best supports your needs.
Each layout is based on a detailed brief
informed by educational and operational
requirements, and prioritises different ways
of using the building for sport, recreation,
and community activities.

Choosing hall layouts

support your school’s learning activities,
gatherings, performances, recreation,

variety of activities.

choose the layout option, or combination

students and day-to-day needs.

Daylight, ventilation & comfort

Natural light

Heating and ventilation for comfort

Services

Standard designs are developed to provide
good natural light under typical site
conditions.

Standard designs aim to provide comfortable
hall and gym spaces year-round, using
simple, efficient systems. The standard
approach combines natural ventilation with
straightforward heating. This works well in
all climates and helps reduce cost, energy
use, and long-term maintenance. We also
consider your site conditions when planning.
In some situations – such as noisy locations
– alternative heating and ventilation systems
may be needed.

We will work with you to understand any
existing infrastructure and services already
in place. This includes discussing your current
systems and any suppliers you may already be
working with, so we can factor these into the
project. Examples include:

During planning, we consider how the
building is positioned on your site — including
orientation, nearby buildings, and trees.
This helps to ensure that spaces feel bright,
comfortable, and usable throughout the day.
In most cases, the standard design will already
meet daylight requirements. Where site
conditions are more constrained, the project
team may review lighting levels to confirm that
the spaces will still perform well.
